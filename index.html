About Me

My Design Philosophy

I am an architecture graduate with a Master's degree in Urban Design, passionate about creating sustainable and people-centred environments. I hold a Master of Urban Design from Newcastle University, United Kingdom (2025–2026), and a Bachelor of Architecture from Bahçeşehir University, Turkey (2021–2025).

Through academic and professional experience at Ryder Architecture, MawsonKerr, and Architenko Brussels, I have developed skills in architectural design, urban masterplanning, landscape integration, site analysis, spatial planning, 3D modelling, and visualisation. Alongside my design background, freelance work in digital marketing and content creation has strengthened my communication and presentation skills. I am particularly interested in the relationship between people, place, and the built environment, with a focus on creating meaningful and resilient spaces.

Community Center

Küçükçekmece, Istanbul, Turkey

post code 34290

This project proposes a community centre that strengthens social interaction and community engagement within the neighbourhood. Responding to the site's topography, urban connections, and surrounding land uses, the design transforms a solid building mass into interconnected public and semi-private spaces. The centre includes a library, exhibition areas, workshops, a café, a gym, and multi-purpose halls organised around open courtyards and communal spaces. By combining cultural, educational, and recreational activities, the project creates an inclusive destination that promotes community well-being and everyday social interaction.

Complex Building

Maçka, Istanbul, Turkey

post code 34367

This project proposes a mixed-use accommodation complex that responds to the site's topography, urban context, and surrounding views while balancing public and private spaces. The design evolves from a simple building mass through a series of strategic additions and subtractions that enhance daylight, circulation, and visual connectivity. Following the natural slope of the site, the stepped form creates terraces, semi-open spaces, and communal areas that encourage social interaction. By combining residential units with shared facilities such as study spaces, workshops, cafés, and recreational areas, the project creates a vibrant living environment that fosters community, comfort, and a strong connection to the landscape.

Architectural School

Bomonti, Istanbul, Turkey

post code 34381

This project proposes an Architectural School designed as a dynamic environment for learning, collaboration, and community engagement. The concept is inspired by the spine, with the building's structural framework remaining visible and acting as the main organising element. Similar to a spine, academic and social spaces are connected to this central core, creating a clear spatial hierarchy and strong visual connections throughout the building. The school accommodates studios, classrooms, workshops, digital labs, a library, and exhibition spaces, organised around open circulation routes and shared learning environments. By combining education with public interaction, the project fosters creativity, collaboration, and the exchange of ideas.

Urban Design

High Level Bridge

Newcastle upon Tyne, UK

post code NE8 2AW

This project proposes an urban design strategy that reconnects Newcastle and Gateshead through the revitalisation of the historic High Level Bridge and its surrounding riverfront. The design focuses on improving pedestrian and cycling connectivity, activating underused green spaces, and creating a vibrant cultural corridor that links heritage landmarks, public spaces, libraries, and recreational areas. Through interventions such as green infrastructure, temporary markets, outdoor learning spaces, cultural activities, and enhanced public routes, the project transforms neglected urban spaces into safe, active, and accessible destinations. By combining heritage preservation, environmental sustainability, and community engagement, the proposal aims to strengthen social interaction, improve spatial justice, and create a memorable urban experience along the River Tyne.

Newburn

Newcastle upon Tyne, UK

post code NE15 8NY

This project proposes an alternative housing development for a 51-hectare riverside site, addressing flooding, affordability, and sustainable growth. Guided by the principle of "flow with water, not against it," the design combines flood-resilient housing with a mix of private, affordable, social, and co-housing models. Green corridors, community spaces, and nature-based water management strategies are integrated to create a resilient and inclusive neighbourhood that promotes social interaction, environmental sustainability, and long-term adaptability.

Ouseburn

Newcastle upon Tyne, UK

post code NE6 1LH

This project proposes a design-code-led regeneration strategy for a former industrial riverside site, transforming it into a vibrant mixed-use destination while preserving its industrial heritage. Guided by the principles of ecological connectivity, industrial identity, and creative diversity, the design introduces cultural spaces, creative workspaces, community facilities, and active public areas that respond to the site's existing character. Green corridors, pedestrian and cycling routes, and riverside public spaces strengthen connections across the site and improve accessibility. By adapting existing structures and integrating landscape and heritage assets, the project creates a distinctive and sustainable environment that supports community engagement and long-term urban regeneration.
